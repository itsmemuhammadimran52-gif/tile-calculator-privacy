Summary

  • Your tile projects, measurements and settings are stored on your device. The app has no account and no developer-run server, and the developer does not receive your projects.

1. About the app

Tile Calculator & Planner is an Android app that estimates how many tiles a floor or wall needs. You enter the size of the surface, the tile size and the grout width, and the app shows a layout preview, the number of full and cut tiles, whole boxes and an estimated cost. Results are estimates only.

2. Information stored on your device

The app saves the following in its private storage on your device so your work is still there when you reopen it:

  • Project names you type and the measurements you enter (surface rectangles, deductions, tile size, grout width, pattern, waste percentage, packaging and prices, currency).
  • Preferences such as the unit system and currency.
  • A count of calculations you completed and small counters the app uses to decide when it may show a full-screen ad or ask you for a Google Play review (for example, whether a review was already requested).
  • A local record that Pro was verified by Google Play, so Pro keeps working when you are offline.
  • Temporary PDF files created when a Pro user exports a PDF (kept in the app’s cache and cleaned up automatically after about 24 hours or when more than five exist).

The developer does not have access to this information. It stays on your device unless you choose to share or save it (see section 6).

3. Backup and device transfer

The app turns Android backup off and its cloud-backup rules exclude all app data, so your projects are not uploaded to Google Drive or any other cloud backup by the app. If you start a phone-to-phone transfer, Android decides whether app data is moved; the app’s rules never allow the Pro verification record, advertising and consent data or temporary PDF files to be copied (Pro is re-checked with Google Play and consent is collected again on a new device). Do not rely on projects moving to a new phone; if you need to keep a result, share or save it yourself.

4. What the developer receives

  • The app has no user account and the developer runs no backend server for the app. The app contains no developer analytics or crash-reporting service.
  • The developer does not receive your projects, measurements, prices or preferences.
  • The developer can see information that Google makes available to developers, such as aggregated advertising reports in AdMob, and sales, ratings, public reviews and technical statistics in Google Play Console. What Google shows is decided by Google.
  • If you contact support by email, the developer receives your email address and whatever you write.

5. Device permissions

The app does not ask you for runtime permissions and does not access your location, contacts, camera, microphone or photos. It writes a PDF only where you choose through Android’s file picker. Android lists a few automatic, install-time permissions that come from Google’s libraries: internet and network state (to load ads and talk to Google Play), the Google advertising ID permission, basic phone state, and background-work permissions used by Google’s advertising library; and the Google Play billing permission for purchases.

6. Sharing and exporting

When you tap Share, the app hands a plain-text shopping list to the Android share sheet and the app you choose receives it. When a Pro user exports a PDF, the file is created on your device and you share it through the share sheet or save it where you choose using Android’s file picker. What the receiving app or service does with that content is governed by that app’s own privacy policy. The developer does not receive these files.

7. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows ads using the Google Mobile Ads SDK (Google AdMob): a banner on the results screen and an occasional full-screen ad when you leave it. Users who have bought Pro do not see ads, and the app does not use the advertising software to show them any.

When the SDK is active, Google may collect and use information from your device as described in Google’s own disclosures, which for this SDK include:

  • your IP address, which can be used to estimate your general location;
  • device and account identifiers such as the Android advertising ID and an app-set ID;
  • information about how you interact with ads and the app (for example taps, ad views and app launches);
  • diagnostic information about the app and the SDK’s performance.

Google uses this for showing ads, measuring ad performance, and preventing fraud and abuse, and states that data is encrypted in transit. The developer does not receive this raw data. The exact data and purposes are determined and may change at Google’s discretion; see the links in section 13.

The app does not mark its ads as directed at children and does not collect information for advertising itself.

8. Your choices and consent

  • Consent form. The app uses Google’s User Messaging Platform. In regions where the law requires it (for example the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, depending on how the ad account is configured), a consent form is shown before any ad is requested. If consent does not allow ads, the app does not request them.
  • Privacy choices. When Google’s consent tool says you must be able to change your choice, a Privacy choices item appears in the menu on the Projects screen, and you can reopen the form at any time to change or withdraw your decision.
  • Device settings. Android lets you delete or reset your advertising ID and opt out of ads personalization in your device’s settings (the exact menu depends on your device and Android version), and Google provides ad controls at adssettings.google.com.
  • Pro. Buying Pro removes ads, and no consent form is shown to Pro users.

9. Purchases (Google Play Billing)

The app offers an optional one-time Pro purchase (no subscription) that removes ads, removes the saved-project limit and enables PDF export. Purchases are processed by Google Play. The developer does not receive your card or other payment details. The app asks Google Play which purchases belong to your Google account, checks the purchase and its signature on your device, acknowledges it to Google, and keeps a local record that Pro is active. Google may make order and transaction information available to the developer in Google Play Console under Google’s terms. Use Restore purchases on the Pro screen if Pro is not recognised. Refunds and payment questions are handled through Google Play.

10. Google Play review prompt

After you have completed a few calculations the app may ask Google Play to show its in-app review prompt, at most once. The prompt is shown and handled by Google Play. The app does not receive your rating or review text, and cannot tell whether you reviewed. Public reviews you post on Google Play are visible to the developer as Google makes them available.

11. Deleting your data and retention

  • In the app: delete a project from the Projects screen to remove it from your device.
  • All local data: in Android settings open Apps → Tile Calculator & Planner → Storage → Clear storage (menu names vary), or uninstall the app. This removes the projects, preferences and local counters. After a reinstall, Google Play can restore the Pro purchase.
  • Retention on your device: project data stays until you delete it, clear storage or uninstall. Temporary PDF files are cleaned up automatically as described in section 2.
  • Google’s records: the app cannot delete information that Google holds, such as advertising, consent and purchase records. Those are kept under Google’s own policies; use your Google Account controls and the pages linked in section 13. Because there is no app account, there is nothing for the developer to delete on a server.

12. Children

The app is a tool for people who are measuring and buying tiles, such as homeowners and contractors. It is not designed for or directed at children, and the developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The app does not collect personal information for the developer from anyone. If you believe a child has provided information through support email, contact the address above and it will be deleted.
